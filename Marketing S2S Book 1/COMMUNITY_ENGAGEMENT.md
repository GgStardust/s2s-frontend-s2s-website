# Community Engagement Strategy

**Last Updated:** January 2025

---

## Overview

Authentic community engagement is critical for reaching the right readers. This strategy focuses on value-first participation, not promotion. The goal is to establish credibility and relationships in communities where consciousness architecture is actively discussed.

---

## 1. Reddit Strategy

### Target Subreddits

#### Primary Targets

**r/consciousness** (1.2M members)
- **Focus:** General consciousness discussions
- **Strategy:** Share insights on structural approaches
- **Frequency:** 2-3 thoughtful comments per week
- **Content:** Answer questions about consciousness architecture, pattern recognition

**r/philosophyofmind** (45K members)
- **Focus:** Philosophy of consciousness
- **Strategy:** Engage in structural vs. abstract discussions
- **Frequency:** 1-2 comments per week
- **Content:** Discuss consciousness as structure, architectural frameworks

**r/systems thinking** (15K members)
- **Focus:** Systems approaches
- **Strategy:** Connect S2S to systems thinking
- **Frequency:** 1-2 comments per week
- **Content:** Systems architecture, pattern recognition

**r/cognitivescience** (45K members)
- **Focus:** Cognitive science research
- **Strategy:** Bridge consciousness architecture to cognitive science
- **Frequency:** 1 comment per week
- **Content:** Structural approaches to perception, cognition

#### Secondary Targets

**r/neurophilosophy** (8K members)
- Smaller, highly targeted
- Deep engagement opportunities

**r/consciousness studies** (varies)
- Academic focus
- High-quality discussions

### Reddit Engagement Rules

**DO:**
- ✅ Answer questions authentically
- ✅ Share insights when relevant
- ✅ Build reputation as knowledgeable contributor
- ✅ Include book link in profile/bio only
- ✅ Participate in discussions without promoting

**DON'T:**
- ❌ Direct self-promotion (banned)
- ❌ Post about your book directly
- ❌ Spam links
- ❌ Create fake accounts
- ❌ Violate subreddit rules

### Reddit Content Strategy

**Value-First Approach:**
1. **Answer Questions:** When someone asks about consciousness structure, provide helpful answers
2. **Share Insights:** Offer unique perspectives on consciousness architecture
3. **Engage Debates:** Participate in structural vs. spiritual discussions
4. **Build Credibility:** Establish expertise through quality contributions

**Timeline:** Start 6 months before launch, maintain ongoing

## 7. Crisis Management

### Potential Issues

**1. Misclassification as Spiritual**
- **Response:** Gently correct with canonical wrapper language
- **Prevention:** Clear messaging, structural language

**2. Accusations of Self-Promotion**
- **Response:** Apologize, return to value-first approach
- **Prevention:** Strict adherence to community rules

**3. Academic Skepticism**
- **Response:** Provide evidence, engage respectfully
- **Prevention:** Build credibility through quality contributions
