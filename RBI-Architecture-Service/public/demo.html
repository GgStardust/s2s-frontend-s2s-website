The Problem: Fraud Detection Costs Are Unsustainable

Primary Pain Point: Massive AI Costs

$36M - $365M+ per year
Financial institutions process millions of transactions daily. Each fraud check costs $0.001-$0.10 via AI/ML APIs:
  • 1M transactions/day × $0.10 = $100,000/day = $36.5M/year
  • 10M transactions/day × $0.10 = $1M/day = $365M/year
This is the single largest operational cost for fraud detection systems.

Secondary Pain Point #1: High False Positives

Current fraud detection systems have 5-10% false positive rates, blocking legitimate transactions. This causes:
  • Customer frustration and lost revenue
  • Increased support costs
  • Damage to brand reputation

Secondary Pain Point #2: Lack of Explainability

Regulators require explainable AI decisions. Black-box AI systems can't explain why they flagged something:
  • Compliance violations and regulatory fines
  • Inability to audit decisions
  • Legal challenges to fraud detection

Secondary Pain Point #3: Processing Delays

Real-time fraud detection needs <100ms response time, but AI systems take 1-5 seconds:
  • Transaction delays frustrate customers
  • Lost revenue from abandoned transactions
  • Inability to scale to high-volume periods

Secondary Pain Point #4: Compliance & Audit Trails

Regulators require mathematical proof for audit trails, not just confidence scores:
  • Black-box AI can't provide verifiable proof
  • Expensive compliance overhead
  • Risk of regulatory penalties
