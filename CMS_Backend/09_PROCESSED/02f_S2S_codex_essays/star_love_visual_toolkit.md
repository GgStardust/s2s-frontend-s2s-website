 Star Love Visual Toolkit: Archetype Glyphs and Constellation Mapping

 Archetype Glyphs (Symbol Set)

Each archetype is represented by a unique glyph that encodes its relational frequency. These glyphs can be used in:

- Session worksheets
- Journals
- Visual meditations
- Group placements (ritual cards, somatic games)

 Proposed Symbols

 Ignitor: Spiral igniting upward from a point (activation flame)
 Mirror: Vertical line mirrored by wave (reflection + distortion pattern)
 Muse: Concentric stars radiating outward (creative pulse)
 Architect: Nested geometric frame (structure within structure)
 Companion: Infinity symbol woven with heartbeat wave (continuity + presence)

Each glyph holds energetic intention and becomes a visual resonance anchor.

 Constellation Wheel (Mapping Diagram)

A circular diagram that places the client's name or sigil at center. Each outer ring holds:

- Archetype
- Primary layer of interaction
- Frequency feedback (Expand / Mirror / Ground / Disrupt / Inspire)

 Usage Instructions

Clients will:

- Place names or initials of connections
- Draw energetic threads between points (open, tangled, complete, evolving)
- Use color or line quality to reflect dynamics

 Optional Elements

- Center circle for personal intention or phrase
- North/South alignment to orient to cosmic directionality (optional ritual layer)
- Space outside the wheel for "incoming" or "dissolving" connections

This visual gives form to the unseen constellation field, turning pattern into presence.

 Mirror Prompt Deck (Inquiry Set)

A set of guided prompts inspired by the Mirror archetype, designed to reveal unseen dynamics, dissolve projection loops, and support clients in becoming their own clearest reflection.

 Use Contexts

- During : sessions to clarify stuck or looping relational patterns
- As a journaling practice between client sessions
- As a closing ritual prompt when completing a relationship
- As a daily card pull or reflection when calibrating to sovereign clarity

 How to Use

- Select – prompts intuitively, or choose based on what archetype you sense is most active
- Write or speak your response without filtering
- Use prompts before/after constellation mapping, or during conflict integration

 Sample Prompts

 Self-Reflection Layer

- What part of myself am I seeing through them?
- What truth have I been avoiding that they are now mirroring?
- What am I holding them responsible for that is actually my own?

 Closure & Clarity Layer

- What frequency did this connection activate in me?
- What wisdom has already been delivered?
- Is there something I am still trying to retrieve or resolve?

 Recalibration Layer

- If I fully honored my sovereignty, how would I respond?
- What would it look like to meet this field without narrative?
- What new signal am I ready to broadcast?

 Optional Format

- Print as cards for ritual pulls
- Include as a scroll segment in a workshop
- Send – personalized prompts after sessions as energetic follow-up

"Mirrors don't lie—but they do distort. These prompts help bring the image into focus."

 Archetype Prompt Deck Expansion

Following the success of the Mirror Prompt Deck, additional decks are created for each archetype:

 Ignitor Prompt Deck (Breakthrough Set)

A collection of prompts designed to catalyze change, spark activation, and support high-frequency shifts in perception, purpose, and relational clarity.

Use Contexts:
- When clients feel a connection is shaking or disrupting their status quo
- To clarify moments of initiation, awakening, or accelerated growth
- During transitions, project launches, or timeline shifts

Sample Prompts:
- What is this person or moment catalyzing in me?
- What am I afraid to change that this connection is daring me to face?
- What new possibility is being revealed through this intensity?
- How do I want to direct this ignition toward something conscious?

 Muse Prompt Deck (Creative Frequency Set)

Designed to open the field of imagination, play, beauty, and inspiration. The Muse deck invites clients into the frequency of lightness, artistry, and untapped sensual genius.

Use Contexts:
- Creative block clearing
- Navigating flirtation, levity, or joy in new relationships
- Vision boarding, project dreaming, or art-based inquiry

Sample Prompts:
- What part of my creative nature is being awakened?
- Where in my life could I invite more play?
- If I created from this connection, what would I make?
- What do I desire to experience more sensually?

 Architect Prompt Deck (Design Alignment Set)

For moments when a relationship is asking for structure, clarity, or collaborative alignment. These prompts help anchor conscious co-creation.

Use Contexts:
- In business partnerships or vision-based intimacy
- Defining roles, timelines, or values
- Clarifying what wants to be built together

Sample Prompts:
- What structure supports the energy we're sharing?
- What values must be honored in this collaboration?
- What am I available to contribute or receive here?
- Where are we misaligned in tempo, vision, or resource?

 Companion Prompt Deck (Continuity & Devotion Set)

Created for long-standing connections, rhythms of care, and sovereign devotion. The Companion deck nourishes grounding, presence, and mutual anchoring.

Use Contexts:
- Long-term partnerships or friendships
- Navigating transitions without separation
- Re-rooting into care during change, grief, or growth

Sample Prompts:
- What does presence look like in this moment?
- What rhythm supports our continuity without obligation?
- Where is care being asked to deepen or simplify?
- What am I tending that wants acknowledgment?

Each deck holds a distinct relational tone. Together, they form a full-spectrum language of star love.
