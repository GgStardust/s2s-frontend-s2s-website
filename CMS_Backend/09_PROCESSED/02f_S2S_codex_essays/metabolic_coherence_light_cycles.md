### Earth–Light Ecology
Every environment shapes its own spectral fingerprint. Forest light filters through chlorophyll, desert light amplifies reflection, urban light fractures into noise. The body reads these variations as syntax, tone, pitch, and punctuation of the planetary conversation.

- Morning: blue–cyan dominance initiates orientation; nervous system primes attention.
- Midday: full spectrum drives throughput; clarity peaks.
- Evening: red/NIR invites repair; parasympathetic coherence rises.
- Night: darkness completes the sentence; melatonin scripts the next cycle.

When artificial light erases night, the sentence never ends; coherence frays. The remedy is contrast, not deprivation: restore the difference between brightness and depth so the body can once again hear time.
