 Archetypes of the Constellation [@archetypes]

Star Love maps relational archetypes as resonance positions [@archetypes]:
- The Anchor: grounding and stabilizing [@Orb_: Temporal Sovereignty].
- The Mirror: reflecting and amplifying light [@Orb_: Photonic Intelligence].
- The Catalyst: sparking change and ignition [@Orb_: Alchemical Current].
- The Bridge: connecting dimensions and beings [@Orb_: Bridging Intelligence].
- The Radiant: embodying transparency and truth [@Orb_: Radiant Transparency].

@scrollstream These archetypes are fluid; one being may embody multiple roles depending on constellation dynamics [@Orb_: Temporal Fluidity] [@temporal_fluidity].
