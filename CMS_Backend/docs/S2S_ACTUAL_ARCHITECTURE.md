- **Research Integration**: Content library search and analysis
- **Thread Management**: Create, switch, persist conversations
- **AI Integration**: Codex-aware responses with CoC validation

#### **2. Field Console** (`/app/creator/field-console/page.tsx`)
**CONTENT MANAGEMENT & INSIGHTS**
- **Resonance Tracking**: Real-time resonance calculations
- **Content Stream**: Recent content with filtering
- **Orb Explorer**: Active orb threads and associations
- **Reflection Panel**: Insight logging and analysis
- **Activity Stream**: Live events and updates

#### **3. Book Compiler** (`/app/creator/book-compiler/`)
**BOOK CREATION & MANAGEMENT**
- **Book Management**: Create, edit, organize books
- **Chapter Editor**: Content editing with AI assistance
- **Source Linking**: Connect content files to chapters
- **Metadata Display**: Purpose, overview, structure, TOC
- **Export Pipeline**: PDF/ePub/DOCX generation

#### **4. Content Library** (`/app/creator/library/`)
**CONTENT STORAGE & RETRIEVAL**
- **Content Files**: Store and manage all content
- **Search & Filter**: Find content by orbs, tags, type
- **Edit Interface**: Modify content with AI suggestions
- **Metadata Management**: Orbs, tags, resonance scores

#### **5. Scrollstreams** (`/app/creator/scrollstreams/page.tsx`)
**SOCIAL MEDIA CONTENT**
- **Card Layout**: Proper card-based UI
- **Quick Capture**: Fast content creation
- **Orbital Suggestions**: AI-powered orb and tag suggestions
- **Social Integration**: Instagram, LinkedIn publishing
- **Status Management**: Draft, published, scheduled

#### **6. Style Training** (`/app/creator/style-training/page.tsx`)
**AI TRAINING SYSTEM**
- **Writing Pattern Analysis**: Sentence structure, vocabulary, voice
- **Orb Integration**: Cross-orb synthesis, undercurrent usage
- **Content Structure**: Operational context, essence statements
- **Training Data**: Learn from your writing style
